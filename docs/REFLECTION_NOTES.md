# Personal Reflection Notes

Use these prompts to capture real experiences while building CodeGuardian. Notes should be honest, specific, and written in your own voice. Do not turn planned work into an accomplishment claim.

## Before building
- Why did I choose this question?
- What did I initially believe a monitor could do?
- What part of the question is uncertain or difficult?

## During implementation
- Which design choice did I make independently?
- What did I try that did not work?
- What evidence caused me to revise a plan?
- How did I balance blocking risky actions against allowing legitimate work?
- What feedback or outside input changed my thinking?

## After experiments
- What result surprised me most?
- What do the raw counts actually show?
- What cannot be concluded from my test setup?
- What would I change in a second iteration?
